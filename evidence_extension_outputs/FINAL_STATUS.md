## Scientific findings and boundaries

The new cohort does not preserve the earlier neural ranking. At primary stone ages 1-10, MineWorld RMSE is 0.768 blocks, history pixels 0.635, VideoMAE-S 0.656, and direction/age 0.211. Paired intervals favor history pixels and VideoMAE-S over MineWorld. These results are reported in the main text and conclusion, not replaced by a favorable narrative.

The new motions include speed changes, acceleration, stops, and reversals, but remain deterministic conditional on their schedules and are still predictable from metadata. The identical-input control tests the information boundary, not the impossible recovery of an unobserved random branch. The experiments do not establish persistent object memory or a general failure of MineWorld. A second layout is included; a second entity is not.
