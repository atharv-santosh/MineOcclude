\section{Evidence-extension design and reproducibility}
\label{app:extension}
All extension files are separate from the earlier studies. The original captures,
predictions, models, and delivered manuscripts are retained. The central transfer,
baseline, and opacity analyses reuse the 25 central identities and therefore do
not constitute an independent replication. The new paired-motion study uses an
independent family split and does not pool its results with the central cohort.

\subsection{Cross-condition decoding}
For each of the five source materials, we use the original five outer identity
folds and four inner rotations. The inner mean identity RMSE chooses
$\alpha\in\{0.1,1,10,100,1000\}$. Constant features are removed using source-training
data only, and all other features are standardized using source-training means
and standard deviations. The fitted intercept, coefficients, feature mask, and
scales are then applied unchanged to the held-out identities in every destination
material. No destination labels or statistics select or calibrate the decoder.
The resulting matrix contains 11,875 predictions. The diagonal reproduces the
earlier corrected-RGB predictions to a maximum absolute difference of
$2.54\times10^{-12}$ blocks. Transfer penalty subtracts the destination-native
RMSE from the unchanged source-decoder RMSE, within identity. Intervals resample
25 identities 10,000 times, seed 94111.

\subsection{Equal-history comparators}
Frame differences concatenate the current $48\times28\times3$ RGB image with
15 consecutive differences in the same resized history, giving 64,512 features.
The optical-flow comparator computes 15 dense Farneb\"ack fields on $96\times56$
grayscale images, with pyramid scale 0.5, three levels, window 15, three
iterations, polynomial size 5, sigma 1.2, and flags 0. Fields are area-resized to
$24\times14$, flattened, and concatenated with the current RGB image, giving
14,112 features. Both receive the exact same ordered 16 recorded images as
MineWorld, and neither receives target state, age, direction, or future images.

VideoMAE-S uses the official Kinetics-400 self-supervised 1600-epoch checkpoint
\citep{tong2022videomae}. Its frozen encoder has 12 blocks, width 384, six heads,
and tubelet size two. Each of the 16 RGB images is resized bicubically to
$224\times224$ without cropping and normalized by ImageNet channel statistics.
With no masking, eight temporal slices of $14\times14$ tokens are averaged in
time, then adaptively pooled to $2\times6$ spatial cells. The 4,608 features enter
the same grouped ridge procedure. This baseline matches history and adds a
pretrained nonlinear encoder, but does not equate architectures, resolution,
pretraining data, or total capacity. The source revision and exact checkpoint hash
are distributed in the provenance files; checkpoint loading uses tensor-only
deserialization. The upstream CC-BY-NC-4.0 license permits the present
noncommercial research use. Neither model is fine-tuned.

Central baseline intervals use seed 94112 and paired MineWorld-minus-comparator
differences use seed 94114, each with 10,000 identity draws. These are descriptive,
pointwise intervals conditional on fitted models, not multiplicity-corrected
significance tests.

\subsection{Fixed-appearance opacity manipulation}
The overlay uses a single stone texture from the first predetermined central
stone capture, frame zero. Its mask is the convex hull of the projected eight
corners of the as-built wall bounds, using the fixed camera and field of view.
The mask is defined by wall geometry, not by a target detector or hidden label.
Every image in the 16-frame history is transformed as
\[
I_{\alpha}(u) =
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{round}((1-\alpha)I(u)+\alpha W(u)), & u\in M,\\
I(u), & u\notin M,
\end{cases}
\qquad \alpha\in\{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1\}.
\]
The target path, actions, mask, texture, and fitting folds stay fixed. Pixels
outside the mask are unchanged. The geometric mask covers every detected sheep
pixel in all 475 scored air frames, and full-opacity frames contain no detected
sheep pixels. Zero-opacity pixel and MineWorld features are bit-identical to the
unmodified air features. We report both per-opacity native fits and unchanged
cross-opacity transfer for MineWorld and history pixels. Residual image energy
relative to the fixed texture is an implementation diagnostic, not a calibrated
object-only visibility measure. Alpha blending is a controlled image manipulation,
not a physically accurate transparent-material renderer.

\subsection{Prospective paired-motion families}
The new roster contains 128 independent families: 64 training and 64 test.
Each partition has 32 families per layout, eight per motion type. Directions are
balanced. Every family contains two variants, and each variant is recorded in
air and stone, yielding 512 capture units and 9,728 scored rows. The four types
are constant velocity, acceleration, stop/resume, and reversal. Motion is
deterministic conditional on the sampled schedule, with no injected hidden process
noise. These trajectories must not be described as unobservable random events.

At 20 Hz, the low-speed parameter is uniform on $[1.4,1.9]$ blocks/s.
For the final low-speed reversal arm, the lower bound is 1.6 rather than 1.4,
following the visibility-admission amendment described below. The
high-speed parameter is uniform on $[2.8,3.5]$, except that constant-velocity
high-speed pilots required a bounded revision to $[2.65,2.8]$. Acceleration is
uniform on $[0.8,1.5]$ blocks/s$^2$, negative for the low variant and positive for
the high variant, with speed clipped to $[0.25,4.5]$. Stop/resume uses a 12-tick
period with eight moving and four stopped ticks, phase zero or six, and moving
speed 1.5 times the sampled parameter. Reversals add a sinusoid of amplitude
3.8 blocks/s and period 12 ticks to the sampled speed, again with phase zero or
six. Motion is held still for ticks 0--19 and after tick 109. Exact per-tick
velocities, positions, start offsets, targets, and seeds are included in the
prospective roster. Starts are adjusted to match the world position at the first
full-occlusion target within each direction and layout.

Engineering pilots cover both layouts, both directions, all four motion types,
and both variants. Admission requires exact schedule replay, complete image/state
joins, zero queued ticks, drained image writers, correct wall construction,
visible sheep in air, and no detected sheep pixels in scored stone frames. The
air-image target check requires centered position discrepancy below 0.15 blocks
and absolute median offset below 0.30. Two fast right-to-left velocity pilots
failed: three sheep-colored pixels remained in their first scored stone frame,
and their offsets were 0.303 and 0.322. Their records are preserved. Eight capture
units were retested after the bounded velocity revision, leaving every admission
threshold unchanged. Pilot outputs never enter production fitting or intervals.
One low-speed stop/resume pilot also lacked visible history at entry. The approach
for this low-speed arm was extended by a complete 12-tick motion cycle and eight
capture units were retested. The scored motion phase, speed distribution, and
admission thresholds were unchanged. Production also checks the visible-history
count at entry and records counts separately at every scored age.
An initial training capture subsequently exposed a slower constant-velocity case
with no visible approach pixels. Before held-out capture or fitting, the approach
was extended uniformly for the low-speed velocity arm to first target at or after
frame 36. Speeds, entry positions, target-relative paths, sample size, and analysis
thresholds were retained. Superseded captures and the original protocol lock are
preserved with a dated amendment; they are not silently pooled into the revision.
A later low-speed training reversal retained only one prior image above the
unchanged 20-pixel visibility threshold. Extending its approach by one complete
12-tick cycle increased this to two, still below the required three. That
approach-only proposal was not admitted to production. Before any held-out
capture or probe fitting, the final amendment instead mapped the same latent
uniform samples to $[1.6,1.9]$ blocks/s for every low-speed reversal and used first
target 48. Matched entry, reversal phase, sample size, and all thresholds were
retained; targets were recomputed for the amended physical speeds. Regular pilots,
explicit 1.6-block/s checks in both directions and layouts, and the original
failing training identity were validated before resumption. All superseded
captures and proposals are preserved and excluded from production fitting.

Each layout has separate training-only four-fold family validation. Both variants
stay in the same fold. The fixed ridge grid and readout are unchanged; there is no
test-set model selection. Native air and stone fits are also transferred unchanged
to the other condition. For this cohort, direction/age OLS is supplemented by
cubic age terms and their direction interactions. A separately labeled privileged
comparator additionally receives motion-type indicators and their interactions.
Oracle last-position and constant-velocity forecasts use only states before full
occlusion, extending those forecasts through the same causal target filter.

The primary interval is ages 1--10; ages 11--15 and 16--19 are secondary, and the
all-age summary is retained. RMSE and MAE are calculated within each variant and
then averaged within family. Bootstrap intervals use 10,000 draws of families,
stratified by layout and motion type, seed 94113. Paired predictor differences use
the same family units. Variant contrast RMSE compares
$(\hat x_{1,t}-\hat x_{0,t})$ with $(x_{1,t}-x_{0,t})$; sign agreement excludes
zero target contrasts. These analyses test whether predictions distinguish the
paired motions rather than merely exploiting a common entry position. They are
not a test of causal use in generated frames.

\subsection{Information-boundary control and artifact access}
A separately labeled constructed control duplicates the exact observed image
history and actions while assigning two alternative hidden futures. The input
hashes and deterministic predictions must be identical. This checks the
information boundary and absence of target-dependent prediction code; it is not
a second physical capture or evidence that the model identifies an unobserved
random branch. In a fully opaque setting with identical observations and actions,
branch-specific position is not identifiable without an additional cue.

The compact reproduction snapshot contains predictions, targets, folds,
configuration, exact schedules, analysis code, and validation receipts. Its CPU
entrypoint reconstructs extension statistics without model weights or GPU access.
Large raw captures and activation arrays are separate from this compact package.
Local reconstruction and anonymous distribution are distinct checks: the existing
anonymous website had not yet been updated to this snapshot at manuscript build
time. Access must be verified before claiming that the new artifacts are available
to reviewers.
