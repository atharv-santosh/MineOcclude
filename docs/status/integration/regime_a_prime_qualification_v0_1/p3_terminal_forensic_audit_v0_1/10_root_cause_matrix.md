## Ranking
1. **H1** — gate definition compares two non-equivalent predicates (primary; sufficient on its own to explain all three FAILs).
2. **H2** — the gate metric is confounded with the swept variable (co-primary; explains the *pattern* across candidates and invalidates any future PASS obtained by going faster).
3. **H3** — ≈3.3-tick render/state lag (real apparatus defect; not the cause of the FAILs, but the largest threat to the scientific programme, and the driver of H5).
4. H4, H7 (gate scope/feasibility), H5 (stale constant → C02/C03 target contamination), H6 (wrong control variable), H8/H11 (specification and process hygiene).
5. H9, H10 — **rejected on evidence**.

Uncertainty deliberately not collapsed: the split between silhouette-extent (w ≈ 0.6–0.8 blocks) and lag
(≈3.3 ticks) within the 1.04-block band is estimated from 12 calibration runs with per-tick motion noise; the
*existence* of both is established, the *exact partition* is ±0.15 blocks.
