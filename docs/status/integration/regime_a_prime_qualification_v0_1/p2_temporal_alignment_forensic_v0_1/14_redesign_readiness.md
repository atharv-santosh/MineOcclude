# 14 — Redesign Readiness Boundary

**Label: EXPLORATORY_FORENSIC_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY.** This document records only the
boundary after bounded repair; it does not draft an authority, choose values,
or authorize collection.

The infrastructure is ready for independent freeze review because it can now
measure provenance, complete path/state scope, physical calibration support,
detection ambiguity, real render/state alignment, and independent visibility
evidence without embedding scientific acceptance choices.

The scientific design is **not authority-ready in this session**. A later
operator-owned authority must explicitly supply, at minimum:

- required alignment directions, speeds, scenes, trajectory/sample support,
  uncertainty method/allowance, and FIR/pure-delay discrepancy rule;
- calibration physical support, endpoint tolerance, maximum internal gap, and
  minimum sample support;
- visibility classification policy and required independent evidence;
- any precision/recall/specificity/FPR, RMSE/R², transition, hidden-bound,
  development-N, or confirmatory threshold;
- exact source commit/hashes and exact path/index mappings.

No speed, gap, visibility fraction, hidden bound, sample count, stage size, or
threshold is recommended here. Stage 1 and all confirmatory collection remain
unauthorized.
